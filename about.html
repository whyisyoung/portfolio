Bio

👋 Hi, this is Limin. I'm a New York based photographer focusing on lifestyle and architecture, with a minimal and peaceful touch. I also work as an AI engineer with a speciality in security. Previously, I got my PhD from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

Interests

📸Photography
Specifically, architecture and lifestyle (interior design, still life, coffee shops, etc.). I started photography in 2014. Starting with an iPhone, then Nikon, and then Leica and film. I don't have a favorite photographer in my mind. But these photographers are classic: Hiroshi Sugimoto and Maya Matsuura.

💃Fashion
I have been addicted into this since 2021. Some of my favorite designers are Martin Margiela, John Galliano, Matthieu Blazy, Issey Miyake, The Olsen twins, Ryota Iwai, Christophe Lemaire and Sarah-Linh Tran, Phoebe Philo, Jil Sander, Yohji Yamamoto, Noir Kei Ninomiya, Uma Wang.

🏛️Architecture and Interior Design
My greatest admiration is reserved for architects. Top architects on my list: Louis Kahn, Zaha Hadid, Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, Le Corbusier, Tadao Ando, Philip Johnson.

Why “Peace”

As time goes by, I realize that softness, calmness, and peace have been marked on almost everything I like and everything I pursue. From books to music, from architecture to fashion, the photos I take, the place I travel to, I find my solace in these qualities. The tender and serene lie in the pastel hues of a sunset painting the sky, the soft dance of water flowing (embraced by slender trees), the melody of a peaceful song (intertwined with the soft glow of scented candles).

I am extrovert, but I can be introvert with all these around (maybe adding a glass/mirror).
