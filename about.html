👋 Hi, I’m Limin — a New York–based photographer specializing in lifestyle and architecture. My work gravitates toward calm, clarity, and detail, capturing spaces and moments with a minimal, peaceful touch.

Photography has been part of my life since 2014. I began with an iPhone, later explored digital and film, and eventually found my voice through a style shaped by Japanese minimalism and sophistication. I’m drawn to clean lines, subtle textures, and quiet elegance — qualities that guide both how I see the world and how I translate it into images.

Beyond photography, I find inspiration in architecture, fashion, and design. From Louis Kahn's timeless forms to John Galliano’s deconstruction, I admire creators who balance simplicity with depth. These influences echo in my own work: light as structure, shadow as texture, stillness as emotion.

For me, photography is a way of finding peace — in a fleeting gesture, a quiet corner, or the glow of a city at dusk. My aim is not just to document but to create a sense of presence, where the viewer can pause, breathe, and see beauty in the understated.

If my work resonates with you, feel free to reach out via whyisyoung.foto@gmail.com or Instagram @whyisyoung.
